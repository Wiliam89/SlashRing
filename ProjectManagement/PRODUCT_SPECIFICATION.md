# Product Vision

SlashRing is a professional guitar processing suite inspired by the classic signal chain associated with Slash.

Its objective is NOT to reproduce only one guitar tone.

Instead, SlashRing provides a complete modular environment where every processing stage can be independently adjusted, allowing musicians to recreate Slash-inspired sounds or design entirely original tones.

The product must achieve commercial quality comparable to modern professional guitar plugins.

# Product Philosophy

SlashRing is NOT an emulation of one song.

SlashRing is a professional guitar processing platform inspired by Slash's workflow while allowing complete creative freedom.
